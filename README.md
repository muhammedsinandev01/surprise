# Romantic Date Invitation Website 💌

A modern, playful, interactive romantic date invitation with a cute pastel pink and cream aesthetic, floating watercolor hearts, micro-interactions, responsive mobile layout, and WhatsApp integration.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Screen 1 — Welcome**:
  - Interactive 3D envelope with animated wax seal (`💌`), opening flap, and sliding love note.
  - Sparkle particles and floating background watercolor hearts.
  - "Hey, pretty girl… 👀" and playful subtext.

- **Screen 2 — The Question**:
  - Adorable animated bear with a pulsing gift heart.
  - "I think you and I should go on a date. What do you say? 🥹"
  - **Playful Dodging "Nope" Button**:
    - Automatically dodges clicks, taps, and mouse hover.
    - Displays rotating funny speech bubbles:
      - *“Excuse me?? 😭”*
      - *“That button seems broken 😂”*
      - *“Give me one more chance 🥺”*
      - *“You sure about that? 👀”*
      - and more!
    - The YES button gently grows larger with each attempt!

- **Screen 3 — She Says YES**:
  - Grand celebration with colorful confetti cannons and heart bursts.
  - 3 Interactive Date Option Cards:
    1. ☕ **Coffee & conversations** (Cozy & Chill)
    2. 🍝 **Dinner & cute vibes** (Romantic & Fun)
    3. 🌅 **Sunset walk** (Sweet & Scenic)
  - Optional custom note input for specific cravings or day preferences.

- **Screen 4 — Final Surprise & WhatsApp**:
  - Polaroid photo frame with illustrated cuddling cats and handwritten caption.
  - Summary of the chosen date plan.
  - **"Send me your YES 💌"** button: Opens WhatsApp with a prefilled romantic confirmation message.
  - Copy to clipboard button for iMessage, Instagram DM, or SMS.

- **Romantic Background Music & Sound Effects**:
  - Background music toggle in the top-right corner (OFF by default).
  - Includes dual audio engine: streams an acoustic romantic track with a Web Audio API synthesized music-box fallback that works offline.
  - Built-in UI sound effects (envelope slide, playful boing on dodge, victory arpeggio on YES, select chime).

- **Mobile First & Fully Responsive**:
  - Optimized for touch targets, smooth scrolling, and dynamic mobile viewports.
